General Guidelines:

  1. The total duration of the examination is 90 minutes (120 minutes for eligible PwBD candidates).
  2. The countdown timer in the top right corner displays the remaining time.
  3. There are 100 multiple choice objective questions across 4 sections:
    • General Science: 40 Questions (40 Marks)
    • Mathematics: 25 Questions (25 Marks)
    • General Intelligence & Reasoning: 25 Questions (25 Marks)
    • General Awareness: 10 Questions (10 Marks)
  4. Marking Scheme: Each correct response awards +1.00 Mark. Each incorrect response incurs a deduction of 1/3rd Mark (-0.33). There is no penalty for unattempted questions.
